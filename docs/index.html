Eight courses, one table. Some are a dish somebody cooks; some are a list you can split, a line each. Swear to what you're bringing and nobody turns up with the same thing twice.

Dress the part

Sopranos and Godfather, not fancy dress. Dark suits, black shirts buttoned high, gold at the wrist. Fur, animal print, sunglasses you don't take off indoors, hair with some height, lipstick that means it. Black does most of the work and gold does the rest. If you own a hat, this is the night for it.
